# Music blog

The release page is a post on a publishing profile. The cover, title, and preview share the opening spread. The article, related posts, and press form the reading column. Listening links and credits have their own rail. On narrow screens the same regions stack, with the title first and the rail after the article.

The music blog uses a light palette: warm paper, dark ink, and a rust accent for playback, arrows, and focus. Fine rules group adjacent links. Georgia sets titles and article copy, while system sans-serif sets metadata and controls. Release format, year, and label follow the title instead of decorating it from above.

Release and regular posts share a profile-scoped URL. The loader adds release details when they exist; the post template keeps the editorial structure without displaying empty sections.
